1. What Is the Quebec City Food Wheel?

The Quebec City Food Wheel is a dedicated Quebec City restaurant picker that randomly chooses your next meal from a curated selection of French-Canadian dishes. I built this tool because reading through endless menus creates decision fatigue, and people needed a faster way to pick dinner.

It features various modes, including Quebec Classics, Bistro & Brunch, Street & Market Eats, Neighbourhood Picks. Whether you want a quick snack or a full meal, the wheel provides an instant decision. This keeps your evening moving without the usual restaurant debate.

2. How Do You Use the Quebec City Food Wheel?

To use the Quebec City Food Wheel, simply choose one of the preset categories like Quebec Classics or Bistro & Brunch, then click the Spin button. The wheel will rotate and land on a random Quebec City dish, instantly deciding your next meal location without any extra steps.

  1. Select a category from the buttons below the wheel.
  2. Click the central "Spin" button to start the animation.
  3. Wait for the wheel to stop and reveal your food choice.
