1. What Is the Office Trap?

This innovative application operates as a strategic name picker game that entirely eliminates traditional guesswork. By utilizing animated digital avatars that bounce randomly around a defined space until caught by a user-placed trap, it guarantees your selection experience remains completely fair, genuinely suspenseful, and thoroughly entertaining.

2. How Do You Use the Office Trap?

Playing Office Trap is remarkably simple and incredibly intuitive. First, verify your participant roster is properly configured in settings. Next, carefully select a strategic location on the digital canvas to place your virtual snare, and watch closely as the animated avatars bounce unpredictably until one is ultimately captured.
