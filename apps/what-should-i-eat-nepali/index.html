1. What Is the Nepali Food Wheel?

The Nepali Food Wheel is a dedicated Nepali restaurant picker that randomly chooses your next meal from a curated selection of Himalayan dishes. I built this tool because reading through endless menus creates decision fatigue, and people needed a faster way to pick dinner.

It features various modes, including Nepali Classics, Mains & Grills, Snacks & Starters, Drinks & Sweets. Whether you want a quick snack or a full meal, the wheel provides an instant decision. This keeps your evening moving without the usual restaurant debate.

2. How Do You Use the Nepali Food Wheel?

To use the Nepali Food Wheel, simply choose one of the preset categories like Nepali Classics or Mains & Grills, then click the Spin button. The wheel will rotate and land on a random Nepali dish, instantly deciding your next meal location without any extra steps.

  1. Select a category from the buttons below the wheel.
  2. Click the central "Spin" button to start the animation.
  3. Wait for the wheel to stop and reveal your food choice.
