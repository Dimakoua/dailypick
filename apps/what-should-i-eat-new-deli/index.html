1. What Is the New Delhi Food Wheel?

The New Delhi Food Wheel is a dedicated New Delhi restaurant picker that randomly chooses your next meal from a curated selection of Indian dishes. I built this tool because reading through endless menus creates decision fatigue, and people needed a faster way to pick dinner.

It features various modes, including Delhi Classics, Street Favorites, International Delhi, Neighbourhood Picks. Whether you want a quick snack or a full meal, the wheel provides an instant decision. This keeps your evening moving without the usual restaurant debate.

2. How Do You Use the New Delhi Food Wheel?

To use the New Delhi Food Wheel, simply choose one of the preset categories like Delhi Classics or Street Favorites, then click the Spin button. The wheel will rotate and land on a random New Delhi dish, instantly deciding your next meal location without any extra steps.

  1. Select a category from the buttons below the wheel.
  2. Click the central "Spin" button to start the animation.
  3. Wait for the wheel to stop and reveal your food choice.
