1. What Is the Shanghai Food Wheel?

The Shanghai Food Wheel is a dedicated Shanghai restaurant picker that randomly chooses your next meal from a curated selection of Chinese dishes. I built this tool because reading through endless menus creates decision fatigue, and people needed a faster way to pick dinner.

It features various modes, including Shanghai Classics, Chinese Favorites, Street & Global Eats, City Dining. Whether you want a quick snack or a full meal, the wheel provides an instant decision. This keeps your evening moving without the usual restaurant debate.

2. How Do You Use the Shanghai Food Wheel?

To use the Shanghai Food Wheel, simply choose one of the preset categories like Shanghai Classics or Chinese Favorites, then click the Spin button. The wheel will rotate and land on a random Shanghai dish, instantly deciding your next meal location without any extra steps.

  1. Select a category from the buttons below the wheel.
  2. Click the central "Spin" button to start the animation.
  3. Wait for the wheel to stop and reveal your food choice.
