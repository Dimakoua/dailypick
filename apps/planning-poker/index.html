    1. What Is the Planning Poker Hub?

    Planning Poker Hub is a collaborative estimation tool for agile teams that lets everyone vote on story points together in real time. I built this because enterprise tools were too clunky and expensive. This provides a sleek, completely free environment for sizing.

    Key Value Propositions & Information Gain: Our Planning Poker Hub is completely ad-free, loads in under 1 second, and requires no sign-ups. It is built as a highly optimized browser-native client, meaning the application runs 100% client-side with zero tracking cookies and completely respects user privacy. Additionally, you can instantly share your custom configurations and live results via a simple URL link, making it vastly superior to competing online alternatives.

    2. How Do You Use the Planning Poker Hub?

    To use Planning Poker Hub, simply create a private room and share the link with your development team. Participants select their Fibonacci cards hidden from view. Once everyone has securely locked in their vote, the scrum master clicks reveal to instantly expose estimates.

    3. What Customization Features Are Available?

    This robust platform offers essential customization features for professional teams. You can seamlessly switch between standard Fibonacci, T-shirt sizing, or custom deck values. Additionally, you can securely integrate your Jira backlog directly into the session to pull active tickets and save estimations.

    4. How Does multiplayer Fibonacci estimation Improve Your Experience?

    Leveraging multiplayer Fibonacci estimation significantly improves your experience by completely eliminating anchoring bias during sprint planning. This powerful feature guarantees that junior developers will not just blindly follow senior developers, resulting in drastically more accurate, honest, and reliable project effort estimates.

    5. How Can You Share and Export Results?

    You can efficiently share and export results by utilizing the built-in Jira integration to push finalized story points directly to your backlog. Alternatively, you can instantly download a clean CSV summary report of the entire session to attach directly to your notes.

    6. Why Should You Use a Planning Poker?

    You should use planning poker because it facilitates incredibly honest discussions, effectively surfaces hidden technical complexities early, builds true team consensus, ensures everyone has an equal voice in the planning process, and ultimately makes your software delivery timelines drastically more predictable.
