1. What Is the Kolkata Food Wheel?

The Kolkata food wheel is a random restaurant picker that helps you instantly decide what to eat in Kolkata. I built this tool because picking between Kolkata biryani, kathi rolls, and traditional Bengali fish curry can be incredibly difficult when you are hungry.

It comes packed with everything from famous street snacks to traditional Bengali comfort food. You can easily switch modes depending on whether you want a quick phuchka or a heavy kosha mangsho dinner.

2. How Do You Use the Kolkata Food Wheel?

You use the Kolkata food wheel by clicking the central spin button to get a random dish recommendation instantly. You can choose from four distinct food categories, such as Street Classics or Bengali Favorites, to narrow down your options before spinning for a final meal.

  1. Select a preset food category from the buttons below the wheel.
  2. Click the prominent "🍛 SPIN FOR FOOD!" button.
  3. Wait for the wheel to stop and announce your random meal suggestion.
  4. If you are not happy with the result, simply spin again.

3. What Customization Features Are Available?

The available customization features include four distinct food presets covering Street Classics, Bengali Favorites, City Eats, and Global Choices. You can instantly swap the wheel items to focus purely on traditional curries, fast street food, or local sweets depending on your current craving.

  • Quickly switch between pre-loaded food categories.
  • Clean interface focused strictly on making food choices.
  • Mobile-friendly canvas wheel that adapts to your screen.

4. How Does a Bengali Food Guide Improve Your Experience?

A Bengali food guide improves your experience by eliminating decision fatigue when you want to eat locally but cannot pick a specific dish. Instead of endlessly scrolling delivery apps or debating with friends, this tool forces a quick, random decision, saving time and getting you to dinner faster.

The specialized presets ensure you only roll for the type of food you actually want. If you want a traditional dinner, you select the Bengali Favorites preset and let the spinner decide between macher jhol and dal.

5. How Can You Share and Export Results?
