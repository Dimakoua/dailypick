1. What Is the Charades Word Generator?

The Charades Word Generator is a fast, web-based tool that instantly provides random words and phrases for acting out. It replaces physical cards by generating thousands of unique prompts across various categories and difficulty levels for your game nights and social gatherings.

I built this tool because I was tired of constantly losing the little paper cards from boxed games and running out of fresh ideas during parties. I wanted a frictionless, infinite supply of high-quality prompts that anyone could access instantly on their phone without needing to download an app.

2. How Do You Use the Charades Word Generator?

You use the Charades Word Generator by selecting a difficulty level and category, then clicking the generate button. A random word appears on screen immediately. You then act out the word for your team while the integrated timer ticks down.

  1. Select a difficulty level (Easy, Medium, or Hard) using the dropdown menu.
  2. Choose a specific category, like Movies or Animals, from the second dropdown.
  3. Press the large "Generate New Word" button.
  4. Read the word secretly, then click the "Start 60s Timer" button to begin your turn.
  5. Act out the prompt before the timer reaches zero!

3. What Customization Features Are Available?

The customization features allow you to strictly control the difficulty and theme of the game. You can easily switch from kid-friendly easy words to challenging, obscure phrases, ensuring the game remains fun and appropriate for whatever group you are playing with.

  • Difficulty Levels: Toggle between Easy, Medium, and Hard prompts instantly.
  • Category Selection: Filter words by themes like Movies, Actions, Animals, or Food.
  • Timer Controls: Start, pause, or reset the built-in 60-second countdown timer.

4. How Does Built-in Timing Improve Your Experience?

Built-in timing improves your experience by keeping all the game elements in one single place. Instead of juggling a phone for the word and a smartwatch for the timer, this tool integrates a large, visible countdown directly alongside the active prompt.

This seamless integration prevents arguments over turn lengths and stops players from cheating the clock. By having the timer prominently displayed next to the current charades word, the acting player can easily pace themselves, leading to much more exciting, fair, and fast-paced party games.

5. How Can You Share and Export Results?

You can share and export results by copying the current word directly from the screen, though charades is generally a live, in-person game. You can very easily share the tool itself with friends by directly texting them the page URL.

Because the generator is web-based, there is no need to export lists or print anything out beforehand. Simply open the page on a tablet or smartphone, pass the device to the current actor, and let the tool manage the prompts dynamically.

6. Why Should You Use a Charades Word Generator?

You should use a charades word generator to save money on physical board games, ensure you never run out of unique prompts, keep turns perfectly timed, and make impromptu game nights happen instantly without requiring any complex setup or mess.
