To use this utility, simply paste your items or names into the large input box, ensuring each item is on a new line. Then, select whether you want to just shuffle the list or group them into teams before clicking the generate button.

  2. Select the desired mode: pure shuffle or team generation.
  1. If making teams, choose the number of groups required.
