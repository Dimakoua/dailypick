1. What Is the Would You Rather Game?

The Would You Rather Game is an interactive icebreaker and decision-making tool that generates a random choice between two options, designed specifically to spark engaging team conversations, warm up dull meetings, and provide quick entertainment for everyone involved. It turns a boring start into an engaging, interactive, and memorable session.

I built this because existing tools were clunky and lacked the ability to quickly write your own custom scenarios on the fly. We needed a simple, ad-free interface. Our Would You Rather Game is completely ad-free, loads in under 1 second, and requires no sign-ups. Because it operates as a browser-native client, it runs 100% client-side with zero tracking cookies, meaning it strictly respects user privacy while allowing you to effortlessly customize and generate prompts instantly.

You can either use the pre-built, curated prompts for instant fun, or you can type in your own specific options tailored to your group or current discussion topic. The tool seamlessly handles the randomness, making it a perfect quick-play activity for any gathering.

3. What Customization Features Are Available?

The Would You Rather tool provides a streamlined, highly customizable experience focused entirely on the content of the choices. You are not locked into our pre-set questions; you have full control over the inputs to match your specific context.

  • Custom Text Inputs: Write any scenario you want for Option A and Option B.
  • Curated Prompt Library: Instantly cycle through dozens of built-in, hand-picked scenarios.
  • Swap Options Button: Quickly flip the positions of your two inputs with one click.
  • Local State Saving: Your custom inputs remain saved on your device even if you refresh the page.

4. How Does The Custom Prompts Feature Improve Your Experience?

The Custom Prompts feature drastically improves your experience by letting you tailor the game exactly to your audience. Instead of relying on generic questions that might fall flat, you can input inside jokes, specific work-related scenarios, or localized content.

This original approach provides immense Information Gain because it transforms a generic game into a personalized tool. It solves the problem of rigid, uneditable generators by giving you complete creative control, making the icebreaker far more relevant, engaging, and memorable for your specific group or team.

To share your results, you can simply read the randomly generated outcome aloud during your meeting or take a quick screenshot of the final choice. Because it saves state locally, you can set up a prompt and then share your screen on a video call.
