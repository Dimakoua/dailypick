1. What Is the Travel Destination Wheel?

The Travel Destination Wheel is a digital spinner designed to randomly select exactly where you should go on your next holiday. It features multiple location categories, instantly settling the classic "where should I travel" debate by choosing your specific destination for you.

I built this tool because my friends and I wasted months arguing over group trip locations until flight prices skyrocketed. I needed a fair, randomized way to make the final decision.

This tool provides a superior experience because it is 100% ad-free, loads in under 1 second, and requires no sign-ups. Built as a browser-native client running completely client-side, it uses zero tracking cookies and respects your user privacy, while allowing you to instantly share your custom configurations via a simple link.
