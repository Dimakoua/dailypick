1. What Is the Chongqing Food Wheel?

The Chongqing Food Wheel is a random restaurant picker that helps you instantly decide what to eat in Chongqing. I built this tool because dining scenes are incredibly vast, and I needed a quick way to choose between traditional hot pot or modern street food.

It comes packed with everything from traditional cuisine to global favorites. You can easily switch modes to match your cravings.

2. How Do You Use the Chongqing Food Wheel?

You use the Chongqing Food Wheel by simply clicking the central spin button to get a random dish recommendation instantly. You can easily pick from distinct food categories in order to narrow down the various options before you start spinning for a final, delicious meal.

  1. Select a preset food category from the buttons below the wheel.
  2. Click the prominent spin button on the screen.
  3. Wait for the wheel to stop and announce your random meal suggestion.
  4. If you are not happy with the result, simply spin again.
