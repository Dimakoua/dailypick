1. What Is the Chongqing Food Wheel?

The Chongqing food wheel is a random restaurant picker designed to help you decide exactly what to eat in Chongqing. I built this tool because I often struggled to choose between hot pot, spicy noodles, and street food, and I wanted a fast, unbiased way to pick a local meal.

It comes packed with authentic local dishes. You can easily switch between different food modes depending on whether you want a quick street snack or a heavy sit-down meal.

2. How Do You Use the Chongqing Food Wheel?

You use the Chongqing food wheel by clicking the spin button to generate a random meal suggestion instantly. You can choose from four distinct food categories, such as Spicy Classics or Local Comfort, to narrow down your options before spinning for a final restaurant or dish recommendation.

  1. Select a preset food category from the buttons below the wheel.
  2. Click the prominent "🔥 SPIN FOR FOOD!" button.
  3. Wait for the wheel to stop and reveal your random meal suggestion.
  4. If you are not happy with the result, simply spin again.
