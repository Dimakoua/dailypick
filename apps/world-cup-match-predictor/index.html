    7. Frequently Asked Questions

    How accurate is the World Cup match predictor algorithm?

    The World Cup match predictor uses a highly sophisticated ELO-weighted algorithm designed to accurately reflect true team strengths. While it cannot guarantee the actual real-world result, it mathematically ensures that stronger teams score more goals over multiple simulations.

    Can I use this match predictor for office sweepstakes?

    Yes, this match predictor is absolutely perfect for office sweepstakes. Instead of everyone safely guessing 1-0 for every single game, you can quickly use the simulation to rapidly generate completely unique, mathematically plausible scorelines for everyone.

    What does it mean when the match predictor says penalties?

    If the match predictor successfully generates a completely tied scoreline during a designated knockout stage fixture, it automatically indicates that the match will realistically proceed to extra time and potentially a thrilling penalty shootout.
