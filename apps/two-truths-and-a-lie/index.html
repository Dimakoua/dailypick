🕵️ Two Truths and a Lie

Guess the lie. Host controls the game.

🎭

How to Play

One person is the Host. Everyone else is a Player. The host writes three statements — two true, one false — and the group votes together.

🏠

Share the Room

You are the host. Share the room link or code so teammates can join, then wait until everyone is connected.

🏠

Joined the Room

You are in the room now. Wait for the host to prepare the round and open voting.

✍️

Create Your Statements

Write three statements, mark the lie, then click Prepare Round to start voting.

👀

Watch the Statements

The host sets up the round here. Once voting is open, you’ll see the statements in this area.

🗳️

Open Voting

Click Prepare Round to enable voting for everyone.

🗳️

Vote for the Lie

When voting opens, choose the statement you think is false. The vote buttons appear on the right.

🔍

Reveal the Answer

After everyone votes, click Reveal. The lie will be highlighted and the team can see the result.

🔍

See the Result

After the host reveals, the lie is shown here. Correct guessers earn points if scoring is enabled.

🔄

Start a New Round

Click Reset to clear the round and begin a fresh one.

🔄

Wait for the Next Round

After the reveal, wait for the host to click Reset to begin the next round.
