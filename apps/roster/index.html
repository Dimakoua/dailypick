1. What Is the Daily Pick Roster?

The Daily Pick Roster is an intelligent, centralized hub specifically engineered to securely manage your core team member list. It completely eliminates repetitive manual data entry by serving as the foundational player directory that powers all interactive games and tools across the entire platform.

By connecting directly to your existing workflows, it guarantees that your participant list remains meticulously organized, highly accurate, and instantly available for any meeting.

2. How Do You Use the Team Roster?

To utilize this tool, simply access the main dashboard and begin manually adding your team members' names into the central list. For enhanced efficiency, navigate to the integrations panel to actively link your external software, allowing the system to automatically synchronize your active users.

  1. Open the central roster dashboard.
  2. Manually input team member names or connect an integration.
  3. Authorize the necessary third-party application permissions.
  4. Verify that your list has successfully synced across all tools.

3. What Customization Features Are Available?

The platform provides exceptional administrative flexibility. You can seamlessly add or meticulously remove individual members, toggle active participation status for absentees, and establish robust direct connections with popular external project management tools to automate your entire daily team maintenance workflow completely.

  • Manual player management
  • Active/inactive toggle status
  • Direct integration with Jira, Trello, and GitHub

4. How Does Jira Integration Improve Your Experience?

A native Jira integration profoundly improves your workflow by automatically pulling in your active developers. I built this unified roster manager strictly to solve the recurring headache of constantly manually updating team member lists across multiple different platforms every time a new employee onboarded.

5. How Can You Share and Export Results?

The centralized roster is inherently shared across your platform account, ensuring all connected tools automatically inherit the exact same synchronized list. Currently, the primary focus is on internal synchronization rather than external exporting, keeping your workflow incredibly tight and completely secure.

6. Why Should You Use a Daily Pick Roster?

You should absolutely adopt this roster system to maintain a single source of truth for your agile team. It completely drastically reduces administrative meeting overhead, guarantees nobody is accidentally left out of activities, and flawlessly streamlines participation across all connected interactive platform apps.

  • Managing a central team list
  • Syncing users from Jira
  • Tracking contributors via GitHub
  • Streamlining Trello user management
  • Maintaining lists for Daily Pick games

How it works

  • Player names are stored locally in your browser via localStorage.
