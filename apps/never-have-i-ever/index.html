1. What Is Never Have I Ever?

Never Have I Ever is a digital version of the classic party game that automatically generates prompts. Players read the displayed statement, and anyone who has performed the action must take a drink, put down a finger, or confess their story to the group.

I built this tool because I was tired of playing this game at parties where people ran out of good ideas after five minutes. This generator provides a massive database of prompts categorized by intensity, completely eliminating the awkward silence of trying to invent a clever question.

2. How Do You Use the Generator?

Playing is incredibly straightforward and requires almost no setup. You select your preferred category, tap the screen to reveal a new prompt, read it aloud to your friends, and watch as the confessions begin.

  1. Open the Never Have I Ever app on your phone or computer.
  2. Select a category (e.g., Party, Dirty, Funny) using the buttons.
  3. Click the main card to generate your first random prompt.
  4. Read the statement aloud to the room.
  5. Players who have done the act take a drink or lose a point.
  6. Click the card again for the next question!

3. What Customization Features Are Available?

This generator allows you to tailor the game's intensity directly to your specific audience. You can quickly filter questions by specific categories, toggle distinct visual themes, and easily copy any prompt to save for later.

  • Category Filters: Choose between Party, Dirty, Funny, Deep, and more.
  • Visual Themes: Clean, distraction-free interface optimized for mobile use.
  • Quick Copy: Use the copy button to grab the exact text of a funny prompt.
  • Endless Mode: The app continuously cycles through hundreds of unique questions.

4. How Do Categories Improve Your Experience?

Filtering by category ensures the questions are always appropriate for the specific group you are playing with. You can select "Deep" for a quiet night with close friends, or switch to "Dirty" for an unpredictable bachelorette party, preventing awkward situations with coworkers or family members.

This targeted content curation is what sets a digital generator apart from trying to remember questions on the spot. You control the exact tone of the game, shifting from mild icebreakers to intense confessions with a single click.

5. How Can You Share and Export Results?

If you encounter a particularly hilarious prompt that you want to share with someone who isn't present, sharing is built right into the interface. Just tap the copy button located near the prompt to instantly copy the text to your device's clipboard.

From there, you can paste the question directly into a text message, WhatsApp chat, or post it directly to your social media feeds to see how your followers respond.
