1. What Is the London Food Wheel?

The London Food Wheel is a dedicated London restaurant picker that randomly chooses your next meal from a curated selection of local dishes. I built this tool because reading through endless menus creates decision fatigue, and people needed a faster way to pick dinner.

It features various modes, including London Classics, International Eats, Trending Street Food, Neighbourhood Picks. Whether you want a quick snack or a full meal, the wheel provides an instant decision. This keeps your evening moving without the usual restaurant debate.

2. How Do You Use the London Food Wheel?

To use the London Food Wheel, simply choose one of the preset categories like London Classics or International Eats, then click the Spin button. The wheel will rotate and land on a random London dish, instantly deciding your next meal location without any extra steps.

  1. Select a category from the buttons below the wheel.
  2. Click the central "Spin" button to start the animation.
  3. Wait for the wheel to stop and reveal your food choice.
