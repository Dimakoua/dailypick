1. What Is the Never Have I Ever Wheel?

The Never Have I Ever Wheel is an interactive digital spinner that randomly selects prompts for the classic party game. Instead of reading from a list, players spin the colorful wheel to land on a spontaneous, unpredictable confession prompt, making your gatherings unforgettable.

I built this version of the game because reading text prompts off a screen can sometimes feel dry. Adding the physical suspense of a spinning wheel, complete with sound effects and anticipation, turns a simple question-and-answer session into an engaging, high-energy event for the whole room.

2. How Do You Use the Wheel?

Using the wheel is fast and requires zero preparation. You just gather your friends, click the spin button in the center of the wheel, and prepare to confess or drink based on the prompt it lands on, eliminating all setup time and maximizing fun.

  1. Open the Never Have I Ever Wheel on your device.
  2. Gather your group around the screen.
  3. Click the central "SPIN" button to start the rotation.
  4. Watch the wheel decelerate and land on a prompt.
  5. Read the prompt aloud from the popup modal.
  6. Anyone who has done the act takes a drink or loses a point!

4. How Does a Visual Spinner Improve Your Experience?

A visual spinner transforms a standard question game into a shared, suspenseful experience. Watching the wheel slowly tick past a controversial prompt and land on a hilarious one builds tension and excitement that a simple text generator simply cannot match, keeping everyone engaged.

This gamification keeps players engaged longer. The physical act of clicking "Spin" gives the active player a sense of agency, making the resulting confessions feel more earned and the resulting laughter much louder.

5. How Can You Share and Export Results?

To share a particularly funny outcome with someone who couldn't make it to the party, the easiest method is to take a screenshot of the winner modal popup. You can then instantly send this image to your group chat, sharing the hilarious moment instantly.

Since the application is entirely web-based, you can also copy the URL directly from your browser and text it to your friends so they can play along remotely on their own devices.
