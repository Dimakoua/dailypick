    How to Play Momentum Mayhem

    Objective

    The goal of the game is to work together with other players to push the red ball into all of the moving blue traps. The game is over when every trap has captured the ball.

    Controls

    Simply click anywhere on the game canvas. A force will be applied to the ball, pushing it away from your click. The closer you click to the ball, the stronger the push.

    The Traps

    The blue circles on the screen are the traps. Each trap is assigned to a player name. When the ball enters a trap, that player's name is added to the "Captured Players" list, and the trap disappears.

    Special Effects

    Capturing a trap can trigger a random special effect that changes the game dynamics for a short period. These include:

    • Speed Boost: The ball's speed is dramatically increased.
    • Size Change: The ball becomes either much larger or smaller.
    • Trap Frenzy: All remaining traps move at a much faster speed.

    Multiplayer

    This game is designed for collaboration! Share the session URL with your friends to have them join your game. All players control the same ball in real-time. You can customize the names of the players/traps in the settings menu.

    1. What Is Momentum Mayhem?

    Momentum Mayhem is a cooperative, browser-based physics game designed specifically for team meetings. Players share control of a single ball, clicking to push it into moving traps associated with team members, making it an excellent energizer for remote retrospectives and stand-ups.

    I built this because traditional virtual icebreakers often feel forced and lack true interactivity. I wanted a quick, low-stakes game where teams actually have to coordinate their actions in real-time, bringing immediate energy and laughter into any remote meeting.

    2. How Do You Use Momentum Mayhem?

    You use Momentum Mayhem by generating a session link, sharing it with your team, and then everyone clicks to push the shared ball into the moving traps. The round finishes once every player's name has been captured by a trap.

    1. Click the "Copy Session URL" button and share it in your team chat.
    2. Wait for your teammates to join the same session.
    3. Click "Start New Game" to drop the red ball onto the canvas.
    4. Click anywhere near the ball to push it toward the blue traps.
    5. Watch the list of "Captured Players" update in real-time until the game ends.

    3. What Customization Features Are Available?

    4. How Does Shared Physics Gameplay Improve Your Experience?

    Shared physics gameplay improves your experience by forcing real-time collaboration rather than solitary decision-making. Unlike turn-based tools, every single click by any player immediately affects the shared ball's trajectory, requiring teams to actively communicate to guide it into the traps.

    This dynamic environment completely eliminates the awkward silence common in remote meetings. By turning a simple task into a chaotic, shared physical interaction, it organically builds camaraderie and wakes everyone up before serious project discussions begin.

    5. How Can You Share and Export Results?

    You can share results by copying the final order of captured players displayed at the end of the game. The session itself is instantly shared via a unique URL, allowing anyone with the link to join the live physics board.

    The game automatically generates a clear, numbered list of who was captured and in what order. This list can easily be highlighted, copied, and pasted into your retrospective notes or Slack channel to record the day's random outcomes.
