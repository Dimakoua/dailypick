    4. How Does Cryptographic Randomness Improve Your Experience?

    Cryptographic randomness guarantees that every single generated line is entirely unpredictable and free from any algorithmic bias. Unlike standard generators that might favor certain sequences, this method ensures your numbers are chosen with the highest standard of digital fairness available today.

    By relying on crypto.getRandomValues(), we eliminate the modulo bias often found in simpler tools. This means no single number from the 1–70 or 1–25 pools has a mathematical advantage.
