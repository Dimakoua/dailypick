2. How Do You Use the Stand-up Wheel?

Open the wheel, confirm your active team members in the side panel, and click SPIN! The wheel decelerates smoothly, lands on the next speaker, and tracks who has already gone so nobody gets skipped.

  1. Add or toggle team members in your Team Roster or the Stand-up Panel.
  2. Click SPIN! to pick the next speaker fairly.
  3. Use the built-in pop-out window or speaker timer to keep updates crisp.
  4. Reset the round for tomorrow's stand-up in one click.
