I built this tool because I wanted a fun way to test my friends' football trivia knowledge. Traditional quizzes were boring, but spinning a wheel to learn about a giant jalapeño named Pique brought the nostalgia back instantly.

7. Frequently Asked Questions

Who was the first ever World Cup mascot?

The first ever official mascot was World Cup Willie, a lion created for the 1966 tournament in England. This groundbreaking character successfully launched a massive merchandising trend that permanently changed how international sporting events are marketed globally.

Are all characters on the FIFA mascots list animals?

No, not all characters on the FIFA mascots list are animals. While lions and leopards are common, historical mascots also include young boys like Juanito, an orange named Naranjito, a jalapeño pepper, and abstract creatures like the 2002 Spheriks.

How random is the World Cup mascot wheel?

The World Cup mascot wheel is completely random, using a standard true random number algorithm. Every single character currently loaded onto the active wheel has an exactly equal mathematical probability of being selected on any given spin.
