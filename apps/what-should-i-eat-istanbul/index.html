1. What Is the Istanbul Food Wheel?

The Istanbul Food Wheel is a random restaurant picker that helps you instantly decide what to eat in Istanbul. I built this tool because dining scenes are incredibly vast, and I needed a quick way to choose between traditional Turkish food or street snacks.

It comes packed with everything from traditional cuisine to global favorites. You can easily switch modes to match your cravings.

2. How Do You Use the Istanbul Food Wheel?

You use the Istanbul Food Wheel by simply clicking the central spin button to get a random dish recommendation instantly. You can easily pick from distinct food categories in order to narrow down the various options before you start spinning for a final, delicious meal.

  1. Select a preset food category from the buttons below the wheel.
  2. Click the prominent spin button on the screen.
  3. Wait for the wheel to stop and announce your random meal suggestion.
  4. If you are not happy with the result, simply spin again.

3. What Customization Features Are Available?

The available customization features include distinct food presets covering Turkish favorites, street food, kebabs, and sweet treats. You can instantly swap the wheel items to focus purely on traditional snacks, hearty main courses, or classic sweet desserts, depending entirely on your current appetite and your exact cravings.

  • Quickly switch between pre-loaded food categories.
  • Clean interface focused strictly on making food choices.
  • Mobile-friendly canvas wheel that adapts to your screen.

4. How Does A Food Picker Improve Your Experience?

A food picker improves your experience by eliminating decision fatigue when you are craving food but cannot decide what to eat. Instead of endlessly scrolling delivery apps or debating with friends, this tool forces a quick, random decision, saving time and ending the debate instantly.

5. How Can You Share and Export Results?

You can share your results by copying the text of your final food recommendation or taking a quick screenshot of the winning dish. Many users take screenshots of the final choice to text to friends or family to instantly settle debates about where they should eat.
