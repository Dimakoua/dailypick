1. What Is the Montreal Food Wheel?

The Montreal Food Wheel is a dedicated Montreal restaurant picker that randomly chooses your next meal from a curated selection of Quebec dishes. I built this tool because reading through endless menus creates decision fatigue, and people needed a faster way to pick dinner.

It features various modes, including Montreal Classics, International Eats, Food Truck Favorites, Neighbourhood Picks. Whether you want a quick snack or a full meal, the wheel provides an instant decision. This keeps your evening moving without the usual restaurant debate.

2. How Do You Use the Montreal Food Wheel?

To use the Montreal Food Wheel, simply choose one of the preset categories like Montreal Classics or International Eats, then click the Spin button. The wheel will rotate and land on a random Montreal dish, instantly deciding your next meal location without any extra steps.

  1. Select a category from the buttons below the wheel.
  2. Click the central "Spin" button to start the animation.
  3. Wait for the wheel to stop and reveal your food choice.
