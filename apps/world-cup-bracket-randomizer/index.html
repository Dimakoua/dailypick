The bracket randomizer uses a weighted probability algorithm based on real-world team rankings and historical tournament data. Stronger teams naturally have a mathematically higher chance of advancing, but genuine upsets are still factored into the simulation for realism.

Yes, this tournament predictor is absolutely ideal for running office sweepstakes. You can quickly use the "Full Bracket" simulation mode to rapidly generate completely unique, fully populated brackets for every single participant in seconds.
