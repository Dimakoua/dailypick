Why Daily Pick?

Daily Pick started as a simple way for one team to decide who goes first in daily stand-up. Today it gives you two clean, ad-free experiences:

  • Inline Spin the Wheel: Edit options directly on the page, load presets (Yes/No, Giveaways, Classrooms), and remove winners after each spin — no roster or sign-up needed.
  • Roster-Powered Stand-up Games: Save your team once in Roster and spin through Stand-up Wheel, Speedway, Patchinko Machine, and more.
  • Agile Team Rituals: Run Planning Poker, Retro Boards, and Morale Checks with Slack /dailypick and custom team branding.
  • 100% Free & Ad-Free: Zero banner ads, zero popups, and instant browser loading.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I spin a custom wheel without setting up a roster?

Open Spin the Wheel, type or paste your choices directly on the page, and hit spin. You can load presets, upload images, remove winners after each spin, and share your wheel via URL.

What is the difference between Spin the Wheel and Stand-up Wheel?

Spin the Wheel is edited inline on the same page for any one-off list, giveaway, or classroom activity. The Stand-up Wheel connects to your saved Team Roster so your team's names are ready every morning across all stand-up games.

Does Daily Pick require an account or payment?

No. Every tool on Daily Pick is free and works without creating an account. Just open the page and start spinning.

How is Daily Pick different from other spinner websites?

Daily Pick is 100% ad-free — zero banner clutter, popups, or video ads. It runs lightweight canvas spin physics directly in your browser, offers PWA offline support, and includes dedicated agile tools like Planning Poker and Retro Board. See our comparisons: Daily Pick vs Wheel Decide and Daily Pick vs Picker Wheel.

What stand-up games work well for remote teams?

All of Daily Pick's stand-up games are browser-based, so they work equally well in-person or fully remote — just share your screen. Fan favourites include the Stand-up Wheel and Speedway turtle race for speaker order, Patchinko Machine for a pachinko-style draw, and Gravity Drift for a space-themed pick.
